Every page below is declared to be in {language}. Read the text a visitor sees on each: headings, paragraphs, buttons, menu items, form labels, the footer, image alt text.

Fail when either is true:
1. **Text in another language:** a heading, a sentence, a button or menu label, a form label or a paragraph that is in another language and was left untranslated (English on a Slovak page, Slovak on an English page). Brand and product names, proper nouns (places, people, car models), international terms (SUV, Wi-Fi, GPS, e-mail, online) and short loanwords used in the language are fine. A language switcher naming other languages ("English", "Slovensky") is fine.
2. **Missing diacritics:** text in {language} written without the language's diacritics where they belong (in Slovak "Pozicovna aut" instead of "Požičovňa áut", "Vase udaje" instead of "Vaše údaje"). One typo is not a failure; a sentence, a heading or a label written without diacritics is. URL slugs and image file names don't count.

Pass when the visible text of every page is in {language}, apart from the exceptions above.

In the evidence, quote the text that is in another language or lacks diacritics exactly as written, and name the page; or say which pages read as {language} throughout.
