These are a car or van rental's home page, fleet pages and pricing or fees pages, as served. Check how prices are shown.

1. Find the prices on the pages: rental prices ("from 46 € / day"), fees, extras, deposits.
2. For each price, is its currency shown with it (€, EUR, kr, DKK, Kč…)?
3. Do the pages say whether the prices include VAT: a note such as "ceny s DPH", "vrátane DPH", "bez DPH", "incl. VAT", "VAT included", "excl. VAT", "Prices with VAT", or a switch between prices with and without VAT? One site-wide note in the header or footer, or one per page, is enough.

Only presence counts. Never judge whether the prices are low, fair or well presented, and don't check whether the VAT arithmetic is right.

Pass when at least one price is shown, every price shown has its currency, and the pages say whether VAT is included. Fail when no price is shown on any page, when a price is shown without a currency, or when nothing says whether VAT is included. In the evidence, quote one price as written and the VAT note, with their pages, or quote what is missing.
