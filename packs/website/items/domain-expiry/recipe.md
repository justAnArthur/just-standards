## Done when
The check passes on production: the site's domain is registered for at least 60 more days, as its registry states it (RDAP, or WHOIS where the registry runs no RDAP, as SK-NIC for `.sk`), and auto-renewal is on at the registrar.

## Make what is missing
Nothing in the repo changes this. Name the domain, its registrar (from the RDAP or WHOIS answer) and the expiry date in the hand-off.

## Hand off only when
Always: renewing a domain needs the registrar account and a payment. Give the domain, the registrar and the date, and ask for auto-renewal to be turned on.
