Find what the site tells a consumer about resolving a dispute out of court (alternative dispute resolution, ADR). It is usually in the terms, the complaints procedure (reklamačný poriadok) or the footer.

Pass when the pages name at least one body a consumer can turn to out of court, or state the consumer's right to it with where to file: for Slovakia the Slovenská obchodná inšpekcia (SOI) or another entity on the list of ADR bodies kept by the Ministry of Economy (for example the Slovenská asociácia poisťovní or a mediation centre), usually citing zákon č. 391/2015 Z. z.; elsewhere the national consumer complaints board or ADR body (for the Faroe Islands the consumer ombudsman or its complaints board, for Denmark Nævnenes Hus, for Germany a Verbraucherschlichtungsstelle). A statement that the operator does not take part in ADR, when it names the body that would be competent, also passes.

An arbitration clause (rozhodcovské konanie, a named arbitrator or arbitration court under zákon č. 244/2002 Z. z., Schiedsverfahren) is not consumer ADR and does not pass on its own: ADR is voluntary for the consumer and run by a body on the national list.

Fail when none of the pages says anything about out-of-court dispute resolution, when the only dispute body named is an arbitrator or arbitration court, or when the only dispute information is a link to the EU online dispute resolution (ODR) platform, which closed on 20 July 2025.

Quote the sentence that names the body, and name the page it is on.
