Find who operates this site, as the site itself states it. Read every page below: the footer of each, the contact page and the opening of the terms.

Look for each of these, stated in plain text on at least one of the pages:
1. the operator's legal name, with its legal form (s.r.o., a.s., GmbH, Ltd, ApS, Sp/f…), not only the brand;
2. its registered address (sídlo): street, town, postcode;
3. its company ID in the country's own register: for Slovakia the IČO (8 digits) and the business register entry (for example "Obchodný register Mestského súdu Bratislava III, oddiel Sro, vložka č. 12345/B", or a trade licence register "Živnostenský register"); for the Czech Republic IČO and the court register; for Germany the Handelsregister (HRB) number; for the Faroe Islands the V-tal or the Skráseting Føroya registration number; elsewhere the local equivalent;
4. its tax or VAT ID: for Slovakia DIČ and IČ DPH (SK + 10 digits) when it is a VAT payer; elsewhere the VAT number;
5. a way to contact it: an e-mail address or a phone number.

Pass when items 1, 2, 3 and 5 are all there, and item 4 is there or the pages give no sign the operator charges VAT. Fail when any of 1, 2, 3 or 5 is missing, or the name, address and IDs belong to a different company than the one the site presents as the operator without saying how they relate (a franchise operator is fine when the site names it as the operator).

Never guess an ID from memory or from outside the pages. In the evidence, quote the name and the IDs exactly as written and name the page they are on, or list what is missing.
