For another kind of business, use its own eight (for a SaaS product: the price, the free plan or trial, what it integrates with, where data is stored, how to cancel, support, onboarding, security).

For each question, look for an answer in the pages below. Only presence counts: is the question answered in plain text (an FAQ entry, a line in the terms, a table)? Never judge whether the answer is good, generous or well written.
