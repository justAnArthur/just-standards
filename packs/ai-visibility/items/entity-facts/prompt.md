Check that the business behind this site states its basic facts in plain text, where an AI assistant or a search engine can quote them. Read the home page (its header and footer too) and the contact page below, and the JSON-LD after this paragraph.

Look for each of these in the visible text (not only inside JSON-LD, an image or a map embed):
1. the business's name;
2. a postal address: street, town, postcode (for a business with several branches, at least the main one or each listed branch);
3. a phone number;
4. opening hours, when the business serves customers at a place or by phone at set times (a branch, a pick-up point, a hotline). An online-only service with no such hours does not need them; say so.

Then compare with the JSON-LD, when there is any: where it states a name, address, phone or opening hours, they must agree with the visible text (the same phone number, the same street and postcode, the same hours). Different spellings of one fact are fine; a different number, street or set of hours is not.

Pass when 1, 2 and 3 are in the visible text, 4 is there or does not apply, and nothing in the JSON-LD contradicts the text. Fail otherwise, naming what is missing or which fact differs (quote both values).
