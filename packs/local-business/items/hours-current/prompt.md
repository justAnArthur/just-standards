Check that what these pages say about opening hours is current, as of today's date (given below). Read every page: banners and notices at the top, the contact page, each location's hours, and any news post about hours.

Look for each of these:
1. a notice about holiday or seasonal hours (Christmas, New Year, Easter, a public holiday, summer or winter hours, a temporary closure, a move or a reopening) whose dates are all in the past, still shown as news on a page: for example "Christmas hours 24.–26. 12. 2025" when today is in 2026, or "summer hours from 1 June to 31 August" when today is in November;
2. a notice that names a past year or season as the current one ("this summer", "in 2024 we are open…") when today is later;
3. regular opening hours that contradict each other for the same location on two pages (the contact page says Monday 8:00–16:00, the location page says Monday 7:00–21:00).

A notice is current when its dates include today or are still ahead, or when it has no dates and doesn't name a past season. A post in a news archive or blog, listed with its publication date as history, is not a notice. Regular weekly hours with no dates are current unless they contradict another page.

Never judge whether the hours are long enough or well presented. Pass when none of 1–3 is found. Fail when any is found. In the evidence, quote the notice or the two conflicting hours exactly and name the page; on a pass, quote the hours or the notice you read.
